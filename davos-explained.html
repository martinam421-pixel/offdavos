Guest card

You paid the tourist tax. Here's what you actually get.

CHF 5.90

Per person · per night · Davos

Current rate for hotels / holiday apartments / camping. Recheck for WEF 2027.

PAYING CHF 8,000 FOR AN APARTMENT DOES NOT MAKE THE CHF 5.90 DISAPPEAR.

Where are you staying?

Hotel

Hotel handles the registration. Ask reception for your digital Premium Card.

Paid apartment / Airbnb

Host/provider needs to handle registration and guest tax. Ask for your Premium Card.

Booked through an agency

Ask who is handling your guest registration and card. Do not assume.

Staying with friends for free

Different category: Private Card.

Own / long-term rent

Different rules apply.

What do I get?

Local buses

Included within the applicable guest-card zones.

Local RhB trains

For a Davos-based card, current core RhB validity covers the Davos local area, including Davos Laret to Davos Glaris within the applicable zones.

Klosters?

Depends on where you are staying and which guest card you have. Check the actual validity.

Mountains + other benefits

Benefits vary by season and product. Check the current official list.

Dog?

Under the current rules, dogs are included with the owner's guest card on covered local bus/train services.

BEFORE YOU BUY A LOCAL TICKET, CHECK YOUR GUEST CARD FIRST. You may already have paid for the journey.
