Piste colours

There is no green.

In Switzerland the easiest piste is blue. Switzerland is being optimistic. Here's the translation.

Blue

You'll probably survive.

Easy. Still a mountain. The CEO in rental boots is on this one. Wave.

Red

Know what you're doing.

Steeper than it looks from the chairlift. Steeper than your LinkedIn says you are.

Black

Your health insurance should know.

Not the place to test your new boots. Or your ego. Or both at once.

Ski route

From Davos Dorf

Parsenn.

The big classic. Up from Davos Dorf with the Parsennbahn, or from Klosters with the Gotschnabahn. If you have one proper ski day, start here.

For

Everyone.

Get there

Parsennbahn, next to Davos Dorf station.

Good to know

Long runs, some all the way down to Klosters.

Pick it if

You only get one day.

From Davos Platz

Jakobshorn.

Right by Davos Platz. Convenient if your meeting mysteriously finishes early.

For

A short escape. Freestyle and snowpark.

Get there

Cable car across from Davos Platz station.

Good to know

The closest mountain to the Platz end of the Promenade.

Pick it if

You have three hours between two meetings.

Klosters

Madrisa.

Over in Klosters. Sunny, family-friendly, and far enough away to forget the Promenade.

For

Families, sunny slopes.

Get there

Train to Klosters Dorf, then the Madrisa gondola.

Good to know

Your Davos guest card doesn't cover the train to Klosters. Buy a ticket.

Pick it if

You need to leave Davos to remember it exists.

Davos Glaris

Rinerhorn.

Closed during WEF week

Further down the valley. Quieter. Good for: fewer badges. It was closed 19–23 January 2026, and this winter's operating times say it closes during WEF again.

For

Families and relaxed skiers.

Get there

Train or bus to Davos Glaris.

Pick it if

You want a day where nobody asks what you do. Before or after WEF.

Above Davos Dorf

Pischa.

Closed during WEF week

The quiet one up the Flüela road. Less piste, more powder. Same as Rinerhorn: closed during WEF in 2026 and again this winter.

For

Freeriders and people who like quiet.

Get there

Bus from Davos Dorf towards the Flüela pass.

Good to know

Off-piste means avalanche risk. Read the bulletin first.

The mountain has opinions

4 pm means 4 pm.

The mountain does not care that your panel ran late. Last lift up is around 4 pm; last lift down shortly after. Your badge does not outrank the mountain.

Check live lift status ↗

Sun down here. Wind up there.

Sun in the valley does not mean sun at 2,600 m. Wind closes upper lifts. Fresh snow off-piste means avalanche risk.
