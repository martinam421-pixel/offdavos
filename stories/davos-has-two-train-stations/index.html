Trains · read this before you board

Your train might split in half.

Davos has two train stations. Except during WEF. Then it has three. And you might be sitting in the half that goes somewhere else.

Save it · the 10-second version

Wrong half: wrong valley. Your meeting starts without you.

Zürich → Landquart → red train → Davos. At Klosters Platz some trains split: one half to Davos, one half to St. Moritz. Check the display in your carriage. Buy your ticket before you board.

The short version: Guide → Arriving in Davos

Under normal circumstances, arriving in Davos is relatively straightforward.

Then WEF happens, and Switzerland quietly adds a train station, splits your train in half, and expects you to know which half you're in.

Everyone is getting off. Don't panic.

You're in Landquart. No, you're not in Davos yet.

From Zürich, every route to Davos changes here onto the red narrow-gauge Rhaetian Railway (RhB). It is not a downgrade.

It is the point.

The train turned red. Do I need another ticket?

Not if your ticket says Davos. SBB to RhB is one journey, one ticket. The railway changed. Your journey didn't.

But: Switzerland has no ticket gates. This is not an invitation. Buy the ticket before you board, in the SBB app or at the machine on the platform. Inspectors are real, fines are real, and "I didn't know" is not a ticket.

You're on the right train. Are you in the right half?

RhB runs some trains as two complete trains coupled together. At Klosters Platz they split. RhB calls this Flügeln, which sounds considerably more romantic than what it means:

Do this

Check the display in your carriage.

Not the platform. Your carriage. In RhB's own example, Davos passengers sit in the rear half while the front continues towards St. Moritz.

Red flag

Everyone gets up at Klosters?

There may be a reason. Listen to the announcement. Then move.

Two stations. Except during WEF.

Davos DorfParsenn side. Unstaffed. Lockers.
Davos Kongress / EisstadionWEF week only. Next to the Congress Centre.
Davos PlatzJakobshorn side. Staffed counter 07:20–18:45, luggage storage, lockers.

Yes. Switzerland builds you another train station for the week. Don't pick the one that sounds most central. Know where you're going before you board.

Between Dorf, Kongress and Platz, the Davos guest card gets you there for free. → The most expensive CHF 5.90 in Davos
