Money / Local reality

The most expensive CHF 5.90 in Davos.

You spent thousands on a flat for WEF week. Switzerland would still like its CHF 5.90.

Save it · the 10-second version

CHF 5.90

Per person. Per night. That's the Davos guest tax. Pay it and you get the guest card: free local trains and buses from your first night, from Davos Laret to Davos Glaris.

No card yet? Information office, Talstrasse 41, Davos Platz.

During WEF, you can spend thousands of francs on an apartment in Davos for a few nights. There will be security convoys outside. Someone nearby will be discussing the future of capitalism.

And Switzerland would still like to talk to you about CHF 5.90.

Per person. Per night.

That is the Davos guest tax for hotels and holiday apartments. The CHF 5.90 itself isn't the interesting part. The machinery behind it is.

During WEF, apartments that are ordinary homes for the rest of the year become some of the most expensive temporary accommodation in Switzerland. Guests arrive through hotels, private hosts, agencies and rental platforms. The small municipal guest tax does not disappear just because the rent has acquired another zero.

And here's the part almost nobody uses properly: the CHF 5.90 is not just a tax. It comes with a card. And the card is a train ticket.

What your CHF 5.90 gets you for free.

Trains

Free RhB trains, Davos Laret to Davos Glaris.

Second class, the whole Davos stretch, as often as you like. Including Dorf ↔ Platz.

Buses

Free local buses in zones 400, 410 and 420.

That's the Davos town network. Get on, show the card if asked.

Side valleys

Staying in Dischma, Clavadel, Monstein, Sertig or Wiesen?

Your card also covers your own side valley, and Monstein/Wiesen guests the train line to Wiesen.

Klosters

Staying in Klosters or Saas?

Different card, different zones: free buses in Klosters, and free RhB trains from Saas all the way to Davos Platz.

Dogs

Your dog rides free too.

Local buses and trains, with a guest card. Davos takes dogs more seriously than dress codes.

From day one

Valid from your first night.

Not from the moment you remember it exists. From the first night you're registered.

What it doesn't get you.

Not included

Landquart, Klosters, Zürich.

Leave the Davos stretch and you need a normal ticket. Buy it before you board.

Not included

The night bus.

invia PickMeUp, the on-demand bus after midnight, does not take the guest card. Pay in the app.

Not included

Your ski pass.

The mountain-railway discounts on the card are listed for summer. In winter, buy a normal ski pass.

Where your card works. On one line.

This is the railway through the valley, top to bottom. Yellow = free with your Davos guest card. Red dashes = buy a ticket.

Before you buy a ticket

Check your card first.

Going from Dorf to Platz? Already paid. You paid it with the CHF 5.90.

WEF bonus

In 2026, all buses were free anyway.

During WEF 2026 the town made all local VBD buses free for everyone, card or not. 2027: not announced yet. We'll update this.

How you get the card.

HotelThe hotel registers you. Ask reception for your guest card.
Paid apartment / AirbnbYour host can register you in advance. If they haven't, do it yourself at the information office.
Booked through an agencyAsk who is registering you. Don't assume somebody else has. This is Switzerland. There is a process.

No card? Walk into the information office at Talstrasse 41, Davos Platz. Pay the CHF 5.90. Get the card. Done.

Hi, I'm staying at [property] from [date] to [date]. Could you please confirm that I've been registered for the Davos guest tax and send me my Davos Klosters guest card? Thank you.

Rented out your flat for WEF? You're a landlord now.

Even if it's just once. Even if you only rent the place long-term yourself. Davos considers you a landlord.

1

Register your guests.

Before they arrive.

2

You're jointly liable.

For their CHF 5.90 a night.

3

CHF 65 per bed, per year.

The tourism levy, once you're registered as a landlord.

4

Same rules for everyone.

Owner, long-term tenant or local. Misuse or evasion is punishable, and evaded tax must be paid back.

We don't give legal advice. Who owes what depends on your situation: ask the official office.
