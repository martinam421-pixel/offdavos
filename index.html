You heard

“You need a badge to walk down the Promenade.”

Actually: No. You need shoes with grip. The badge is for the buildings. The ice doesn't check.

You heard

“Your guest card is a souvenir.”

Actually: It's a train ticket. Davos Laret to Davos Glaris, free, from your first night. You paid CHF 5.90 for it.

You heard

“Taxi is the fast option.”

Actually: During WEF, the taxi is how you spend time, not save it. Motorcade, checkpoint, traffic. Your feet win.

You heard

“A drink will keep you warm.”

Actually: It makes you feel warm. You lose heat faster. Physics doesn't attend receptions.

You heard

“The last bus has gone.”

Actually: There's an on-demand night bus. It doesn't take your guest card or cash. Download the app before 01:40.

You heard

“Someone important will notice your shoes.”

Actually: Nobody looks at shoes. Everybody looks at who just fell over.
