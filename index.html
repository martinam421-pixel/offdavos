Survive CH

Switzerland in six rules. Read it on the train up.

01 · Time

08:21 means 08:21.

Swiss trains leave on the minute. Your meeting may run late. The train will not wait for it.

02 · Trains

Your train may split in half.

Being on the right train is not enough. You need the right half of it.

How it works →
03 · Geography

Davos Platz ≠ Davos Dorf.

Same Davos, two stations, opposite ends of town. Book the wrong one and you get a walk in the snow.

See the map →
04 · Plugs

Your charger may not fit.

Swiss sockets take slim two-pin EU plugs, but not the chunky round German ones. Bring an adapter. Everyone else forgot theirs.

05 · Water

Tap water is free and excellent.

Drink plenty of it. At 1,560 m, the second glass of wine counts like the third.

06 · Language

Say "Grüezi".

That's hello. Everyone here speaks English, but greeting people in the local word gets you a lot further.

Stories

Money / Local reality

The most expensive CHF 5.90 in Davos.

It is the smallest number on your hotel bill, and the one nobody reads. It is also the one that gets you a free bus.

How it works
  • The Gästetaxe (Kurtaxe). Every overnight guest aged 12 and over pays CHF 5.90 per night in a hotel or holiday rental. Group lodgings and youth hostels: CHF 4.00. Children 6 to 11 pay nothing but are still registered.
  • What you get back. Hotel and holiday-rental guests receive the Davos Klosters Premium Card. Ask for it at check-in.
  • The free bus. With the card, local buses are free within the defined fare zones. Side valleys (Dischma, Clavadel, Monstein, Sertig, Wiesen) only count if you stay there.
  • Not included. The train between Davos and Klosters is not listed as part of the card.

Sources: Davos guest tax rates · davos.ch guest card

Getting there / Trains

Your train may split in half.

You can do everything right, board the right train at the right minute, and still wake up on the way to St. Moritz.

How it works
  • Since December 2022 the Rhaetian Railway runs some trains from Landquart as two or more units coupled together.
  • At Klosters Platz the train splits. One part continues to Davos, the other to St. Moritz.
  • Find your half. The destination is written on the driver's cab and on the doors, and screens next to the doors show it too. Board the part that says Davos Platz.
  • The warning sign. A symbol of one point with two arrows splitting off marks these trains in the SBB app and at stations. If you see it, check your carriage.

Source: Rhaetian Railway (RhB)
